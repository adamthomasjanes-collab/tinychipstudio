## Capacity and decisions

Starter has three sub-accounts. Reserving one for Tiny Chip Studio and one for Craig’s Spray Foam Equipment Finance leaves one other slot. Actual account allocation and readiness were not accessible in this task. Two or more additional Growth clients are capacity-blocked under that reservation; rows showing them are hypothetical economics only. Never combine unrelated client contacts to bypass this limit. Care reporting and forms do not require a client CRM account in the current implementation.

Under the working estimate, the extra $100 Growth revenue adds $50 recurring labor + $15 usage + $13 commission/percentage processing = only $22/month before platform allocation and additional onboarding. At full planned-slot utilization, allocating all $97 to that one additional Growth client makes its economics especially tight; internal/Craig costs cannot be treated as free. Confirm who funds the platform and each occupied slot.

The existing 30 minutes/week allowance averages 26 hours/year versus 6 hours/year at the proposed 30 minutes/month. At the illustrative $50/hour that is a $1,000/year difference per fully utilized Care client. Existing signed agreements keep their allowance; do not model those clients using the proposed new-client policy. Growth’s proposed 60 minutes/month is 12 hours/year. Approve scope, response boundaries, onboarding effort, usage caps and separate quotes before accepting work.

Build & Own’s starting price is not equivalent to a year of managed service. At $1,200 and a hypothetical $50/hour, 24 hours already consume the entire price before processing, overhead and profit. Qualify small builds, budget revisions and quote complex work separately. Existing-site care needs its own technical review and labor budget.

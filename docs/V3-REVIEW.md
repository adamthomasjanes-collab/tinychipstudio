## FAQ copy

### What does $197/month include?

$197/month with a 12-month initial agreement. $0 upfront build fee for qualifying projects. $2,364 total first-year payments. Custom design, generally up to five pages, hosting and SSL, maintenance, backups, monitoring, basic technical SEO, contact forms, direct support and a monthly website summary. GA4 and Search Console are set up where applicable. Forms notify your business and include a basic ‘we received your message’ acknowledgment, configured and tested during onboarding. We agree the delivery channel and any usage costs first. Multi-step follow-up sequences and lead management belong to Growth.

### What does $297/month add?

Website + Growth adds lead management to the agreed Website + Care foundation: organized contacts and inquiries, follow-up sequences, appointments, reminders, review requests and clearer opportunity reporting. We set up your inquiry and follow-up system around your business. We agree on the features, usage limits and launch timeline before work begins, then configure and test your workflows during onboarding. Proposed qualifying terms: $0 upfront build fee, 12-month initial agreement, $3,564 in first-year payments.

### Can I buy my website outright?

Yes. Build & Own starts at $1,200 once, with no required care subscription. The agreed custom deliverables transfer after full payment, subject to third-party licenses. You choose your hosting and future provider; growth services can be scoped separately.

### Can you work with an existing website?

Yes, subject to a technical review. Website Care starts at $99/month for narrower technical care and minor updates. Follow-up help can be discussed separately, with availability and scope confirmed first. No new build, CRM or full Growth package is automatically included. WordPress hosting is outside our active scope.

### How much time is included for content updates?

New-client proposal: 30 minutes of minor content updates per month for Website + Care, replacing the existing public allowance of 30 minutes per week; 60 minutes per month for Website + Growth. Adam’s approval is required before publication. Existing signed agreements are unchanged. New pages, substantial redesigns and custom features need a separate quote. Scheduling and any rollover rules must be agreed in writing.

### Are there extra usage or AI charges?

Email, SMS, phone and other usage are not unlimited. Any included allowances, limits and additional charges must be agreed before activation; no numeric usage allowance has been approved yet. AI receptionists, advanced AI, high-volume campaigns, paid advertising, full-service SEO and complex integrations require a separate proposal and approval.

### Will I need to learn CRM software?

You should not need to become a software expert. We will agree what you want to handle yourself, what support is included and what access or guidance you need. Client workspaces and workflows are subject to availability and verified setup; they are not automatically available with every inquiry.

### What kinds of websites qualify?

Straightforward small-business websites, generally up to five core pages. We review content, functionality, integrations and timing first. Complex commerce, custom applications, extensive copywriting and substantial integrations need a separate proposal. We agree revisions and a realistic schedule before work begins.

### Why is there a 12-month commitment?

The qualifying $0 upfront arrangement includes design and development in a continuing service relationship. Website + Care has a 12-month initial agreement and $2,364 in first-year payments. Website + Growth proposes the same initial term with $3,564 in first-year payments. These are ongoing services, not simply financing a $1,200 project.

### Who controls my domain, content and website?

Your domain and the content you provide remain yours. Build & Own custom deliverables transfer after full payment, subject to third-party licenses. For monthly plans, the timing and extent of website/source-code rights and offboarding deliverables must be defined in the written agreement before commitment.

### What happens if I leave or the initial year ends?

The written agreement provided before you commit defines early exit, any agreed buyout, service choices after the initial year, offboarding and transferable licenses. We will walk through those details with you before you sign, including any costs and what you receive when services end. Your domain and supplied content remain yours.

### What happens after I contact you?

We read your inquiry, reply by email and talk through your business, current site and goals. You receive a clear scope, availability, timeline, costs and written terms before deciding. Your inquiry is not a subscription or a commitment to buy.

### Do you guarantee more customers?

No. We help businesses make a strong impression, handle inquiries and understand what is happening. We do not guarantee lead volume, search rankings, customers, sales or revenue.

Privacy, plainly.

Last updated: October 2026

What we collect

When you send the project form, ask for a call, or ask us to look at your scorecard results, we receive what you typed: your name, email, phone (if you add it), current website (if you add it), the options or answers you picked and your message. If you email or call us, we receive what you send.

What we do with it

We use it to reply to you and to talk about your project. We don’t sell it, rent it or add you to a mailing list.

Who else handles it

Form submissions are processed by the company that hosts this website so they can be delivered to us. If you book a call through our scheduling link, the booking is handled by Calendly under their privacy policy. Email is handled by our email provider.

Cookies and tracking

We use Google Analytics and Microsoft Clarity to see how the site is used: which pages and buttons people use, how far they scroll, and anonymous recordings of visits with anything you type masked. They set cookies to do this. We use what we learn only to make the site clearer; we don’t run ads, and advertising features are turned off. You can block these cookies in your browser and the site still works. Your browser may also remember which hidden chips you’ve found and whether you closed Chip’s tips; that stays on your device.

Your choices

Want us to delete what you sent? Email tinychipstudio@gmail.com and we will.

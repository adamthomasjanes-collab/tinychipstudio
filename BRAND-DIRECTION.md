# Brand direction

## Chip
- A white computer chip: square package, ink outline, short even leads on every side, a bevelled pin-1 corner and a lime pin-1 dot. Friendly face: tall eyes, pink cheeks, a lopsided grin.
- Arms and legs are bent leads. He never has a dark face.
- Pixel-honest: drawn on a 32 × 32 grid (16 × 16 for tiny sizes), animated with frame swaps, never smoothed, rotated by fine angles or blurred.
- He is the guide, not the hero. He points at the next step, celebrates the visitor's progress and stays out of the way of reading and forms.
- The crew (Byte, Pixel, Glitch, Boot, Bit) are the same chip in soft body colours. They exist for delight and stay hidden.

## Logo
- Lockups: stacked (Chip + two-line wordmark) and one-line (mini Chip + wordmark), for light and dark, in `public/brand/`.
- The wordmark is drawn on Chip's pixel grid: 9 units tall, 2-unit strokes. Scale it in whole-pixel steps where possible.
- Lime is the accent, never the logo's background, except on the app icon.

## Palette and type
- Ink #0b0d11, paper #f4f1e8, lime #c7ff58, blue #5c89ff, pink #ff69bd. Chip white #fffdf6.
- Barlow Condensed for display, Space Grotesk for labels, Inter for reading.
- Typography: nonbreaking space between the first two and last two words of each sentence; "Tiffin, Ohio" never splits. Large titles colour their second half.

## Voice
- Plainspoken and owner-led, spoken as "we". Visitors are the hero; the studio is the guide.

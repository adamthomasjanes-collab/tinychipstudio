### Compact Instagram phone typography

At the owner's request, the studio homepage now selects smaller type before its first paint for Instagram phone sessions. It recognizes the Instagram user-agent token, or an Android WebView with an Instagram web or app referrer. The CSS applies through 700px. At default settings the hero is 32px, section headings are 30px, project names are 16px and reading text is 14px. Input fields use 16px. The type scale follows enlarged browser defaults.

At 393 × 700 the published homepage measured 27,360 CSS pixels tall and the hosted draft measured 22,701, about 17% shorter. The Chromium audit found no visible content text below 14px or beyond the left or right viewport edge at seven widths from 320 to 430px. Facebook and standalone Chrome control sessions kept the standard phone scale. Emulated sessions supply these measurements. The new draft still needs a physical Pixel 7 check inside Instagram.

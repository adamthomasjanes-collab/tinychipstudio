# Tiny Chip Studio — repositioning and mobile/tablet QA

Completed local implementation and review November 1, 2026. **Not deployed.** This report supersedes earlier offer and QA documents. Public production remains unchanged by this work.

## Critique and resulting changes

The site's distinctive type, pixel characters and real portfolio are strengths. The weakest experience was the space between phone and desktop: the header tried to carry too much navigation, adjacent columns became cramped, and fixed guide content could compete with the information being read. The buying decision also needed a clear hierarchy and consistent terms across the homepage, city pages and metadata.

The revision keeps the brand and portfolio. Tablet navigation now collapses before it collides with the logo; the menu has its own viewport-bounded scroll area. Tablet hero, process, client feature and service layouts get deliberate space. Long names wrap rather than forcing overflow. Chip remains, with tablet tips opened on request and the dock hidden during form editing. The phone headline uses balanced wrapping. Repeated FAQ answers were removed; required commercial questions remain.

The flagship is Website + Care at **$197/month with a 12-month initial agreement**, **$0 upfront build fee for qualifying projects**, and **$2,364 total first-year service payments**. Build & Own remains a genuine from-$1,200 one-time option without mandatory maintenance. Existing-site Website Care starts at $99/month after review, with a narrower scope. The form gathers business context and lets visitors select their approach without losing their draft.

1. Open from the bio. Capture the first screen before scrolling. Check the headline, offer disclosure and “See if my business qualifies” CTA. Compare text and line breaks in Facebook and standalone Chrome on the same phone and font setting.
2. Scroll down and back up as the toolbars contract and expand. Check section headings, spacing, sticky header and quick-contact dock. Open and close the menu at the top and deep in the page. Reach its last link in landscape.
3. Follow Work, Pricing and FAQ. Check the destination heading clears the header. Switch projects and tap and drag across each comparison. Scroll vertically starting on an image. The page should move and the split should stay where you left it.
4. Open a case study, inspect its gallery and close it. Open the external project link and return. Check the page, back navigation, and the ability to return to Instagram. Reload a shared anchor, visit a city page and return to the homepage.
5. Try the primary CTA and a pricing CTA. Check the intended option, focus a contact field, type synthetic text, dismiss the keyboard and reach the submit button. Check the final CTA and footer. Exercise validation without sending an inquiry.
6. Rotate and return to portrait. Check larger system text, pinch zoom and reduced motion. Record any clipped text or controls hidden behind app chrome.
7. Repeat from a DM and a Story link where available. For each surface check redirects, landing, refresh, internal navigation, external handoff and return to the app. Repeat on an iPhone and in Facebook and Messenger as independent sessions.

For a failure, record the exact taps, viewport/orientation, scroll and keyboard state, expected and observed result, screenshot and copied diagnostics. Retest on the same device and entry surface.
